DESTINATION SPOTLIGHT

Amsterdam in spring: a city made for wandering

Updated April 2026 · Best time to visit: March to May

Spring softens Amsterdam. Canal-side terraces reopen, tulips colour the parks and the longer evenings make it easy to move between galleries, neighbourhood cafés and quiet waterside streets without rushing.

The city rewards a loose itinerary. Begin with one museum or landmark, then leave space to follow a canal, stop at a market or take a tram into a neighbourhood you had not planned to see.

THE ESSENTIALS

A considered city break

A few practical choices make Amsterdam substantially easier: stay central enough to walk, use trams for longer hops and reserve the city's most popular museums before you arrive.

01

Getting around

Central Amsterdam is compact enough to explore on foot. Trams are useful when the weather turns or you want to cross the city quickly, while cycling suits confident riders who understand local bike-lane etiquette.

02

Where to stay

Choose the canal belt for atmosphere, De Pijp for food and a neighbourhood feel, or Jordaan for narrow streets, independent shops and easy access to the historic centre.

03

What to prioritise

Pair one major museum with a slower experience: a canal walk, Noord ferry ride, neighbourhood market or an unhurried evening beside the water.
