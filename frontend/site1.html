TRENDING NOW · MUSIC

Why everyone is singing “Du bist gut genug”

By WAVES Culture Desk · 6 min read

A German-language song became instantly recognisable online before many listeners knew what its words meant. The surprise? Its viral hook translates to something genuinely lovely: “You are good enough.”

The line comes from Gut genug by KitschKrieg, Blumengarten and Shirin David. Its journey from misheard meme audio to sincere affirmation is exactly the kind of internet plot twist we like.

THE DEEP DIVE

How the trend unfolded

Four quick reads on the joke, the translation and why the sound stuck.

INTERNET CULTURE

First came “Doobie Scoot Canoe”

English-speaking listeners initially had no idea what the German hook meant. Instead, they jokingly heard “Du bist gut genug” as something close to “doobie scoot canoe.”

The mondegreen spread through memes, reaction videos and short-form clips.

THE MEANING

Then people translated it

The joke changed once people discovered the actual message: “You are good enough.”

LYRIC EXPLAINER

What are the lyrics actually about?

The song pushes back against the feeling that you need to become richer, prettier, more successful or more impressive before you deserve to feel worthwhile.

Its repeated message is simple: self-worth is not another thing you have to earn.

SOCIAL MEDIA

From joke audio to affirmation

One person might use the sound for the misheard lyric joke; another for a friendship edit, a personal milestone or a confidence video. That contrast helped it travel.

WHY IT WORKS

You don’t need to speak German

The phrase is short, memorable and melodic. The emotional payoff arrives twice: first through the sound, then again when the listener discovers what it means.
