SATURDAY FEATURE

Riverside Market: breakfast, baskets and a long wander.

Saturday · 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM

Start the weekend with local food stalls, handmade goods and independent makers beside the river. Come early for coffee, stay for lunch and leave enough time to browse slowly.

It is an easy event to drop into for half an hour, but the best version is unhurried: a lap of the stalls, something warm to eat, and a second walk through once you know which makers caught your attention.
