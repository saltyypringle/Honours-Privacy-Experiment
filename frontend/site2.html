Destination Spotlight: Amsterdam in Spring

Updated April 2026 | Best time to visit: March to May

Explore canals, museums and cycle routes. Amsterdam offers a relaxed mix of culture, neighbourhoods and open-air spaces for long or short stays.

Getting Around

Use the tram network, walk central districts or rent a bike for short trips between landmarks.

Where to Stay

Choose from canal-side hotels, boutique guesthouses or efficient mid-range city stays.

What to See

Anne Frank House, Van Gogh Museum, canal cruises and local markets remain reliable highlights.
